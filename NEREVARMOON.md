# NerevarMoon engine on openmw-rtx

Branch `nm-rtx`: the tag `rtx-v0.4.5` of xorza/openmw-rtx (OpenMW 0.53, Vulkan ray tracer) plus one squash commit of the NerevarMoon engine additions from branch `nm-jolt` (`f4bec41444..88c0458a58`, OpenMW 0.51 line).

Lua API added by the fork: `types.Actor.knockDown/isKnockedDown/isKnockedOut`, `types.Actor.setTimeScale/getTimeScale`, `types.Actor.sever/isSevered/isRagdoll/setLowerBodyLocked`, event `NMHitResult`, `animation.getBoneTransform/hasBonePhysics/setHeadHidden`, `animation.addVfx` option `offset`, `camera.setStaticHeadTracking`, `world.physicsImpulse/physicsSetDynamic/isRagdoll`, `core.weather.setSoundGain(gain)`, `core.weather.setRainLook(streak, haze, mx, my)` (RTX only: carried through `WeatherResult` and `Rtx::WorldReading` to the tracer). Engine side: Jolt Physics ragdolls and knockdown (`[Physics] jolt`), dismemberment, bone physics chains (`<model>.physics.yaml`), Bodyview (`Mask_PlayerHidden`), `[Lua] lua timing log`.

Patches to the ray tracer: the eye's view mask skips `Mask_PlayerHidden` (`renderingmanager.cpp`) and the world walk places it as `Rtx::InstanceClass::ShadowOnly` on `MASK_SHADOW_ONLY`, the sprite bit, which the eye's ray drops and every other ray keeps (`mwrender/rtx/classmasks.hpp`, `components/rtx/shaders/scene.h`): the hidden head still shadows the ground; an occluded window keeps the game running (`components/sdlutil/sdlvideowrapper.cpp`); a blended sheet's own emissive colour keeps the rasterizer's level instead of the tracer's `EMISSIVE_INTENSITY` gain of 8 (`Rtx::sheetGlowShare` in `components/rtx/scene/material.hpp`, applied by `materialresolver.cpp`), so Morrowind's sparks, smoke, blood and dust stop lighting the room; glow maps and opaque glowing surfaces keep the gain; a lamp's intensity stops growing at a recorded radius of 512 while its reach still follows the record (`sIntensityRadiusCap` in `components/rtx/mirror/lightbuilder.cpp`), so the content's fill lights of 768 to 4096 units (`red_3000`, `lava_light_4096`, the `blue water ref` records beside every waterfall) tint instead of flooding; the rain haze from `core.weather.setRainLook` scales the air's extinction by `1 + 1.5 * haze` (`describeWorld` in `components/rtx/world/frameworld.cpp`); its streak strength draws rain streaks on the lens after the tone pass (`RainLensPass`, `shaders/display/rainlens.comp`, the 0.51 `nm_rain.omwfx` formulas): on a frame with streaks of 0.01 or more the curve writes the pass's own image and the pass writes the shown picture, otherwise nothing is dispatched.

CI: `.github/workflows/nerevarmoon-rtx.yml` builds the Windows package on every push to `nm-rtx` and publishes the release `nerevarmoon-rtx-build-N`.
